Join the Lab

If you’re curious about animal behavior and cognition, I would love to hear from you.

Undergraduates

Hunter undergraduates can work in the lab only by enrolling in independent research in psychology for course credit. Lab members get hands-on research experience: coding behavior from video, managing data, reviewing literature, and helping design experiments. No prior research experience is needed; curiosity, reliability, and attention to detail matter most.

To apply, email me with your year and major, why you’re interested in the lab, and the semester you’d like to enroll.

Prospective MA & PhD students

I welcome inquiries from prospective graduate students interested in animal cognition, urban wildlife, or communication. Please email me and include:

  • Your academic interests
  • Your career goals
  • Specific questions or hypotheses you'd like to work on in hyenas or coyotes
  • What unique skills or attributes you would contribute to the lab
  • Your CV
  • An unofficial transcript
  • An example of your scientific writing

Postdocs

I don’t currently have funding for a postdoctoral position, but postdocs interested in applying for fellowship funding with me as a mentor are welcome to reach out.
